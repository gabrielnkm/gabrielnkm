## Hi there 🙋🏻

I'm [Gabriel](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gabrielnkm/), a graphic designer and a developer based in Brazil.

I design and build digital experiences.

I like turning ideas into interfaces, websites, and interactive experiences that not only look good, but actually work.

My work sits somewhere between design and development. I care about visual details, user experience, clean code, and the little interactions that make a product feel alive.

My main tech stack is React, TypeScript, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and Tailwind. On the creative side, I work with Figma, Photoshop, After Effects, and other tools to bring ideas from concept to reality.

I enjoy building things from scratch, experimenting with new technologies, and finding the sweet spot between good design and good engineering.
